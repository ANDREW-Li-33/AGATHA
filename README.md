14 Incident types:

Dynamic Hazards:
SUDDEN_CUT_IN: Vehicle unexpectedly enters ego lane/path with limited clearance (including opposing-direction encroachment).
HARD_BRAKING: Leading vehicle decelerates abruptly enough to demand an evasive response.
PEDESTRIAN_INTRUSION: Pedestrian unexpectedly enters or crosses the vehicle's path.
CYCLIST_INTRUSION: Cyclist unexpectedly moves into or across the vehicle's path.
CROSS_TRAFFIC_CONFLICT: Cross traffic enters or conflicts with the ego vehicle's path.

Construction Disruptions:
CONSTRUCTION_LANE_CLOSURE: Work zone blocks an otherwise available travel lane.
TEMPORARY_LANE_SHIFT: Work zone redirects traffic away from normal lane alignment.
TEMPORARY_TRAFFIC_CONTROL: Temporary worker, sign, cone, or signal changes traffic rules.
CONFLICTING_LANE_GUIDANCE: Temporary work zone markings conflict with permanent lane paint.
WORK_ZONE_ENCROACHMENT: Construction worker, machinery, or equipment intrudes into the travel lane.

Road Obstructions:
STOPPED_VEHICLE_OBSTRUCTION: Disabled or stopped vehicle blocking the lane.
DOUBLE_PARKED_OBSTRUCTION: Unusually parked vehicle obstructing traffic flow.
ROAD_DEBRIS: Fallen or loose materials in the roadway.
OTHER_PHYSICAL_OBSTRUCTION: Any other stationary physical obstacle affecting travel.
