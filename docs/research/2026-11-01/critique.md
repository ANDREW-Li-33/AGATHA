# DESIGN DOC ISSUES
- [4 and 6: SceneWindow as a Stage 1 output] Stage 1 reads as a one-shot 2 FPS export, but Pass 2 needs any time range at 5 to 10 FPS and Stage 4 needs full-rate data. Pre-baked windows would force a rewrite when Stage 2 arrives.
  FIX: Make two per-segment full-rate tables the Stage 1 product. frames.parquet holds every frame and selected camera: frame_timestamp_micros, t_s, pose_timestamp, world_from_vehicle, image pose and gap flag. tracks.parquet holds every lidar_box row unfiltered, plus world position and derived fields. SceneWindow becomes a pure function, build_scene_window(segment, t_start, t_end, fps, cameras, profile). Render on demand and cache as <context>/<camera>/<frame_timestamp_micros>__<profile>.jpg so windows and rates share frames. This is the doc's FrameManifest and full-rate trajectories made explicit, not a scene database.
- [6: time fields in SceneWindow and IncidentReport] One 'timestamp' hides several times. frame_timestamp_micros is the first TOP scan and the join key, vehicle_pose sits near mid-spin, and nominal centre-pixel capture is 37.5 ms (FRONT_LEFT), 50 ms (FRONT) and 62.5 ms (FRONT_RIGHT) after it. A float timestamp_s in source_frames cannot be matched exactly to a frame. Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04838 ; https://github.com/waymo-research/waymo-open-dataset/issues/464
  FIX: Every frame record carries frame_timestamp_micros (int64), t_s = (ts - t0)/1e6 with t0 stored on the segment record, camera, and a measured capture_offset_ms from pose_timestamp. Windows and start_s/end_s use t_s. Add frame_timestamp_micros to source_frames so Stage 2's referential check is exact equality. Never join on floats. Calibration joins use segment plus camera only, because those tables have no timestamp key.
- [5.2 and 5.3: resampling] Spacing is irregular and frames can be missing: one published segment has 191 frames with seven gaps near 200 ms, and no source bounds the count. Every-Nth-row sampling drifts. 2 FPS and 5 FPS are not nested on the 10 Hz index: 20 of the 40 2 FPS frames are not in an every-second-frame set (my calculation). 10 FPS means every frame and is the ceiling. Near a gap, two grid points can select one frame. Source: https://github.com/waymo-research/waymo-open-dataset/issues/158
  FIX: Select the nearest timestamp to a grid anchored at segment t0, not per window. Deduplicate, and report requested_fps, achieved_fps and max_gap_ms. If Pass 2 must re-show a frame Pass 1 cited, force-include it. Sort by timestamp before anything else, and report gaps rather than fail on a fixed frame count.
- [5.2: 20 second window arithmetic] The window list only works for exactly 20 s, while segments end near 19.7 to 19.8 s (about 198 frames on average). At 2 FPS the windows hold 10, 10, 10, 10 and 8 samples: 48 images per camera, 40 unique, last sample at 19.5 s (my calculation). A 1 s overlap guarantees that only events up to 1 s sit wholly inside one window, while the cut-in template allows 0.5 to 6.0 s lane changes, so many cut-ins will be split. That works against the recall goal. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.01485
  FIX: Compute windows from window_s, stride_s and the actual last timestamp, and close the final window at the last frame. Merge any tail under 1 s into the previous window. Keep 5 s and 4 s as the default, and add track-only context to each SceneWindow (start with 2 s either side, no extra images). Test stride 2.5 s on the labeled set: 7 windows, containment up to 2.5 s. Set truncated_start and truncated_end on tracks cut by segment edges.
- [4 step 3: projection transform] 'Using the corresponding calibration' reads as extrinsic-only. Labels live in vehicle_pose's frame and each camera has its own image pose. Using the frame pose instead gives about 30 px on FRONT_LEFT and FRONT_RIGHT and 86 px on side cameras at 25 m/s and 15 m on axis (simulated), which fails the check for three cameras without architectural changes. Depth is x, not z. The extrinsic is camera-to-vehicle, and images keep their lens distortion. Sources: https://github.com/waymo-research/waymo-open-dataset/blob/master/src/waymo_open_dataset/wdl_limited/camera/camera_model.cc ; https://github.com/waymo-research/waymo-open-dataset/issues/834
  FIX: Use one signature from day one: project(p_vehicle, T_w_vf, T_w_vi, T_v_c, intrinsics), with p_c = inv(T_w_vi * T_v_c) * T_w_vf * p_v in float64, then forward distortion. Do not undistort images in Stage 1. The only extrinsic-only path is camera_synced_box. Read width and height from calibration. Return u, v, depth and validity so Stage 4 masking can reuse it.
- [4 step 3: clipping and visibility] 'Clip projections to image boundaries' cannot remove ghost boxes. With the fixture coefficients the distortion polynomial folds back past r of about 0.9 and turns negative near 1.35. A corner 3 m ahead of the camera and 3.5 to 3.9 m to the left, which is an adjacent-lane car beside the ego, lands inside the image at u of about 410 to 1323 (my calculation). cv2.projectPoints has no such guard. Waymo guards with r_d in [0.8, 1.2]. Dropping a box when any corner fails is too strict for partly visible objects. Source: https://github.com/waymo-research/waymo-open-dataset/blob/master/src/waymo_open_dataset/wdl_limited/camera/camera_model.cc
  FIX: Sample each 3D edge at 8 to 16 points. Keep only samples with x above a small positive bound and r_d in [0.8, 1.2], then clip to the image. Sampling also follows edge curvature near image borders. Add a unit test where a synthetic box beside the ego draws nothing. Do not treat a projected box as visible: projection ignores occlusion.
- [4: camera timing and rolling shutter] The doc is silent on camera-LiDAR timing. Global-shutter projection with the stored image pose is exact near one column only (about u=1150 in one sample). At 25 m/s it is off by about 23 to 31 px at the image edges at 15 m and 50 to 58 px at 7.5 m (simulated), which is typical cut-in geometry. A crossing object adds about 31 px at 15 ms of timing gap. Extrapolating boxes by 50 ms would be wrong, about 103 px. Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.07705 ; https://github.com/waymo-research/waymo-open-dataset/issues/544
